# Image Credits

Septum Rental v3 uses Wikimedia Commons imagery for the demo catalog.

The image URLs are stored on each vehicle in the API. The linked Commons
file pages contain the author and exact Creative Commons / public-domain
license information.

Real-world examples include:

- Porsche 911 GT3 RS — Wikimedia Commons, `Porsche 911 992 GT3 RS.jpg`
- Porsche 911 GT3 RS — Wikimedia Commons, `2024 Porsche 911 GT3 RS 992.jpg`
- Ferrari 296 GTB — Wikimedia Commons, `Ferrari 296 GTB.jpg`
- Ford Mustang GT — Wikimedia Commons, `2024 Ford Mustang GT.jpg`
- Mercedes-Benz E-Class — Wikimedia Commons, `2024 Mercedes-Benz E-Class E220d AMG Line.jpg`
- Land Rover Defender 110 — Wikimedia Commons, `Land Rover Defender 110 (48802785268).jpg`
- BMW R 1250 GS — Wikimedia Commons, `BMW R 1250 GS (1).jpg`
- Vespa GTS 300 — Wikimedia Commons, `Vespa GTS 300 - 3 May 2026.jpg`
- Mercedes-Benz Sprinter — Wikimedia Commons, `Mercedes Sprinter.jpg`
- MAN TGX — Wikimedia Commons, `MAN TGX (52839928664).jpg`
- Airbus H125 — Wikimedia Commons, `Airbus H125 (aircraft).jpg`
- Sunseeker yacht — Wikimedia Commons, `Sunseeker.motoryacht.predator.arp.jpg`

Fictional/game-inspired entries use clearly marked representative category
imagery from Wikimedia Commons; they are not presented as exact photographs
of the fictional model.
